# Water budget terms

This page compares OpenH2O's water budget terms with two outside references. The
first is the California Department of Water Resources' *Handbook for Water Budget
Development: With or Without Models*. The second is the set of budget columns that
an IWFM (Integrated Water Flow Model) groundwater model writes out. For each OpenH2O
figure, the table gives the calculation, the nearest handbook term, the nearest IWFM
column, and how closely the three match.

OpenH2O calculates its figures in acre-feet for one field and one month. The
handbook and an IWFM model work with larger areas: a water budget zone in the
handbook, and a subregion in an IWFM model.

The handbook is a draft. The copy the Department of Water Resources publishes is
marked "DRAFT," and we did not find a final version to cite. Page numbers on this page are
the handbook's printed page numbers.

The IWFM columns come from the C2VSimFG v1.5 model package, the fine-grid version of
the California Central Valley Groundwater Surface Water Simulation model. Its budget
files were written by IWFM's root zone package version 4.11, and later IWFM versions
can name some columns differently. Several rows also cite the crosswalk in section 7
of the handbook: Figure 7-55 and sections 7.4.4 and 7.4.5. The Department of Water
Resources based that crosswalk on an older sample IWFM model.

## Term by term

| OpenH2O term | How OpenH2O calculates it | DWR handbook term | IWFM column | How they compare |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crop ET | OpenET's satellite estimate of the field's evapotranspiration for the month (`gross_et_af`). In a month the field is marked fallow, OpenH2O counts it as 0. | Evapotranspiration: "Volume of water entering the atmosphere through the combined process of evaporation from soil and plant surfaces and transpiration from plants." (Table 1-1, p. 9) | Ag. Actual ET (-). The handbook's Figure 7-55 matches Evapotranspiration to the sum of Ag. Actual ET, Urban Actual ET and Native & Riparian Actual ET. | The handbook's term covers all the land in a budget zone. OpenH2O's figure covers the crop on one field. |
| ETAW | Crop ET minus effective precipitation (`net_consumptive_use_af`). | ETAW: "ET of applied water (ETAW) is the amount of irrigation water needed to meet total crop ET in excess of effective precipitation." (section 3.2.1, p. 52) | Ag. ETAW | OpenH2O's calculation matches the handbook's definition. The IWFM column has the same name. We did not check how IWFM calculates it. |
| Effective precipitation | Calculated from GRIDMET rain by the method chosen on the Methodology Settings page (`effective_precip_af`). The default method is USDA-SCS (TR-21). | Effective precipitation: "Effective precipitation is the amount of precipitation stored in the root zone that is available for crop ET." (section 3.2.1, p. 52) | Ag. Effective Precipitation | The names match. We did not check how IWFM calculates the column. |
| Delivered | The canal water that the ledger's `surface_diversion` rows record as delivered to the field in the month. OpenH2O deducts the canal's losses before it divides a headgate's total among the fields. | Surface Water (SW) Delivery: "Volume of surface water delivered to a water budget zone." The definition goes on to say that a delivery is smaller than the diversion by the amount lost to conveyance seepage and evaporation along the way. (Table 1-1, p. 11) | Ag. Deliveries (-). The handbook's Figure 7-55 matches Surface Water Delivery to the sum of Ag. Deliveries and Urban Deliveries. | OpenH2O's figure and the handbook's term match closely. Each counts the water that reaches the land after conveyance losses. |
| ETAW met by surface water | The delivered water times the field's irrigation efficiency, or the month's ETAW, whichever is smaller. OpenH2O stores the full product as `surface_water_af`. The multiplication by the irrigation efficiency is not a setting. | None found. | None found. | OpenH2O defines this figure itself. |
| Applied water not consumed | The canal water the ledger records as delivered to the field, less the part its crop used (`surface_water_af` minus `over_delivery_af`), plus groundwater extraction minus ETAW met by groundwater (`deep_percolation_gw_af`) on a field with a well and no meter reading. OpenH2O sums both over the period's calculation runs. | Percolation: "Volume of applied water and precipitation that travels from the root zone to the unsaturated zone of the aquifer; this water then travels either vertically into the groundwater system or horizontally into the surface stream system." (Table 1-1, p. 10) | Ag. Percolation (-) in the root zone budget. The matching line in the groundwater budget is Deep Percolation (+). | The handbook's term covers applied water and rain together. OpenH2O's figure covers canal water and pumped water, and it is calculated from the irrigation efficiency and the month's ETAW. |
| ETAW met by groundwater | On a field with a well and no meter reading for the month, ETAW minus ETAW met by surface water (`final_af`, which OpenH2O writes to the month's calculated ledger row). | None found. | Ag. ET from Groundwater is the nearest by name. | We did not check how IWFM calculates the column, so the two may differ. |
| Groundwater extraction | On a field with a well and no meter reading, OpenH2O divides ETAW met by groundwater by the field's irrigation efficiency (`gw_extracted_af`). On a field with a meter reading, or with a share of its well's meter reading, OpenH2O uses that figure. OpenH2O charges this figure against a groundwater allocation. | Groundwater (GW) Extraction: "Volume of groundwater pumped (extracted) from the underlying aquifer(s) for use within the water budget zone. It does not include groundwater export, stored water extraction, and stored water export." (Table 1-1, p. 9) | Ag. Pumping (-). The handbook's Figure 7-55 matches Groundwater Extraction to the sum of Ag. Pumping and Urban Pumping. | OpenH2O's figure matches the handbook's term closely. It is an estimate on a field without a meter, and a measurement on a field with one. |
| Surface water in excess of ETAW | The part of the delivered water times the field's irrigation efficiency that is above the month's ETAW (`over_delivery_af`). When rain alone exceeds crop ET, the surplus rain is not part of this figure. A setting on the Delivery Settings page controls how OpenH2O treats this water. | None found. | None found. | OpenH2O defines this figure itself. The field's water balance counts it under Uses, in Applied water not consumed, together with the rest of the delivery that is not multiplied into ETAW. |
| Canal evaporation | A share of each month's direct-use diversion at a point of diversion, after subtracting any water returned to the stream (`evaporation_fraction`). When a point of diversion uses the default losses, the share is 1 percent for an open canal and 0 for a pipe. | Conveyance Evaporation: "Volume of water evaporated into the atmosphere from conveyance facilities, other than streams, during water delivery." (Table 1-1, p. 9) | Non-recoverable Loss, in the diversion budget. Section 7.4.4 of the handbook names the same column. | OpenH2O's figure matches the handbook's term. OpenH2O deducts the share from the headgate total before dividing the rest among the fields, and does not credit this water to any zone or field. |
| Canal seepage | A share of each month's direct-use diversion at a point of diversion, after subtracting any water returned to the stream (`seepage_fraction`). When a point of diversion uses the default losses, the share is 25 percent for an unlined canal, 5 percent for a lined canal and 0 for a pipe, and 0 when the lining is not stated. The defaults come from FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 61 and the published water management plans of two irrigation districts with mostly lined canals. | Conveyance Seepage: "Volume of water recharged to the groundwater system from the conveyance facilities, other than streams, during water delivery." (Table 1-1, p. 9) | Recoverable Loss, in the diversion budget. Section 7.4.5 of the handbook names the same column. | OpenH2O's figure matches the handbook's term. OpenH2O deducts the share from the headgate total before dividing the rest among the fields. It records the seepage in the water budget of the zone that contains the point of diversion, outside the zone's Available groundwater, and does not credit a water account unless Delivery Settings say otherwise. A zone is OpenH2O's record of one management area with its own allocations. If no zone contains the point, OpenH2O records it in the zone of the field that received the most water that month. If neither is in a zone, OpenH2O does not record the seepage in any zone. |
| Spill | A share of each month's direct-use diversion at a point of diversion, after subtracting any water returned to the stream (`spill_fraction`). The share is 0 unless the organization sets it, and the canal's lining does not change it. | No term in Table 1-1. The handbook's guidance on return flow says to count operational spills as Return Flow (p. 143). | None found. | OpenH2O deducts the share from the headgate total before dividing the rest among the fields, and does not credit this water to any zone or field. The handbook counts the same water as return flow. |
| Return flow | The part of a diversion that the organization records as returned to the stream (`returned_af` on the diversion record). | Return Flow: "Volume of applied water that is not consumptively used and flows to the surface water system. It includes treated wastewater discharges to the surface water system." (Table 1-1, p. 10) | Ag. Net Return Flow. The handbook's Figure 7-55 matches Return Flow to the sum of Ag. Net Return Flow and Urban Net Return Flow. | The two match in part. The handbook's term covers applied water that is not consumptively used. OpenH2O's figure can also include water that passed through without being applied, such as a hydropower release. |
| Water use recorded, no supply reported | On a field with no well, the part of the month's ETAW that the canal water on record does not meet (`unmet_demand_af`). | None found. | Ag. Shortage (=) is the nearest by name. | The two are calculated differently. In the IWFM column headers, Ag. Shortage equals Ag. Supply Requirement minus Ag. Pumping, Ag. Deliveries and "Ag. Inflow as Surface Runoff". OpenH2O's figure is crop ET minus effective precipitation and minus ETAW met by surface water. The figure reports what the records show, and it says nothing about what the grower did. |

A water year finalized before OpenH2O stopped estimating canal deliveries can still hold an estimated delivery. In that year it is counted in Delivered and in Applied water not consumed, and it is not part of ETAW met by surface water or of Water use recorded, no supply reported.

Most fields named in the table are on `CalculationRun` in `accounting/models.py`.
The three canal-loss shares are on `PointOfDiversion`, and `returned_af` is on
`DiversionRecord`. Both of those models are in `surface/models.py`.
