Two layouts are recognized by their column headers: the state's own Water Use Reported export (APPL_ID, YEAR, MONTH, DIVERSION_TYPE, AMOUNT) and an operator's own book (a headgate or point column, a date or month column, and a volume). Any other header layout is refused, naming the headers seen. A record for a (point, month, type) already on file is skipped, never overwritten.
