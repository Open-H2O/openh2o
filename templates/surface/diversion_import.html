Columns each layout carries

The layout is recognized by its headers; a file with neither set is refused, naming the headers it found.

The state's Water Use Reported export

Column Required What it holds
APPL_ID Yes Application Number of a water right on file; its point is used when the right has only one
YEAR Yes Report year
MONTH Yes Month number, 1 to 12, placed in a calendar month by the reporting-year setting on Delivery Settings
DIVERSION_TYPE Yes DIRECT or STORAGE; USE rows are settled on the preview; COMBINED is refused
AMOUNT Yes Acre-feet
APPL_POD No Point on file, by name; used before APPL_ID
calendar_month No YYYY-MM; used in place of YEAR and MONTH
A record already on file Skipped, never overwritten, for the same point, month and type; one the platform estimated is replaced

A ditch tender's book

Column Required What it holds
local_name, headgate, point or name Yes Point on file, by its local name or its name
date or month Yes YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY-MM; the record's month
acre_feet or af One of three Acre-feet
volume and unit One of three Unit af, gallons, ccf, mg or cfs_hours; converted to acre-feet
flow_cfs and hours One of three Converted to acre-feet at 1.9835 acre-feet per cfs-day
type No direct, storage or use; blank reads as direct
returned No Returned to stream, acre-feet
A record already on file Skipped, never overwritten, for the same point, month and type; one the platform estimated is replaced
