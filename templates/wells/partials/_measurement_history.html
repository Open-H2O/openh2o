A voided or replaced read or visit stays in date order, struck, with one line under it (the corrected one sits directly above), and is in no count and no total. A finalized water year's header ends in "Finalized" for every role. Up to three tables, two to a row, in this order: each current meter's totalizer reads (ONE table per meter, grouped by water year with its own closing subtotal row -- footer A, no tfoot, rule 7), the visits (levels a person measured by hand or imported: a depth, or the reason none was read) and a logger's monthly depth-to-water digest (grouped, with a net-change row closing each year; levels are not amounts, rule 5). All come from wells/measurement_history.py, newest first; nothing is computed here. A table, not a chart: this page loads no charting library and gains none. `history_rows` (measurement_history.history_rows) names the tables of each row. Each row is its own .history-grid, because the grid is a two-row subgrid and a second row of columns in the same grid would overlap the first.
