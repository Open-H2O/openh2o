{% comment %} The site-wide demonstration notice. WHY IT IS SITE-WIDE AND NOT PER-PAGE. The existing `_demo_marker.html` pill is good at what it does — it disambiguates one value that reads like a real legal action — but it is small, it sits beside a specific field, and it appears on six surfaces. The risk it does not cover is the one that matters most here: someone screenshots a data table from this public demo and presents it as a real regulatory record. A label that can be cropped out, or that never rendered on that page at all, is no defence. This renders at the top of the content area on every page, so any capture of the page carries it. WHY IT NAMES BOTH ANSWERS. "This is a demo" is not, on its own, the honest sentence for this deployment. The Merced demonstration mixes two kinds of data: published public record (a real water system, real EPA facilities, real state laboratory results) and invented rows built to show how the accounting works (the agricultural wells, the ledgers, the allocations, the account contacts). Saying only "sample data" would misdescribe the first; saying only "real data" would misdescribe the second. So the banner says both and links to the page that goes source by source. Flag-gated on SiteConfig.demonstration_mode, which reaches here through core.context_processors.site_config. A real agency deployment has the flag off and emits nothing at all — not a hidden element, nothing. Design: `.card-raised` with no coloured left stripe, which is the house way to lift a passage (DESIGN.md). Deliberately NOT gold: gold is for primary actions in this design system, and a notice is not an action. Deliberately not a bespoke tinted callout box either — that is the generic look DESIGN.md tells us to avoid. {% endcomment %}{% if site_config.demonstration_mode %}
