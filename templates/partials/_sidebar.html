Do not add a link by hand: add a NavEntry to the owning module's ModuleSpec instead, or a deployment that switches that module off will still show a dead link. Everything outside the loop (the brand, Home, the Help section, the mobile-collapse script) is not module-owned and stays written out longhand. Sections group the pages by what their records are about. The settings section is never drawn here: its entries are the rows of the Settings page, opened from the top bar. Which ONE link is lit is decided once per request, for every link including Home and Help, by `core.modules.lit_entry` (the longest owning prefix wins; Home and the Help pages are `FIXED_NAV_ENTRIES` there). The script after the sidebar writes the reader's choice to the `sidebar_drinking` cookie. An entry's own `visibility` is a predicate applied inside its section, written below as empty guard branches ("hidden" cases first, render in the {% templatetag openblock %} else {% templatetag closeblock %}) so the link markup appears exactly once. An entry that opens a write flow carries `writes=True` in the module registry, and the loop leaves it out of a viewer's sidebar.
