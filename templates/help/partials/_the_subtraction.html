Once a month has been calculated for a field with a well and no meter reading, this page works through that field's numbers. Until then, here is the arithmetic in words.

  1. Start with crop ET for the month, OpenET's satellite estimate in acre-feet.
  2. Subtract effective precipitation, estimated from gridMET rainfall. The result is ETAW.
  3. Subtract the delivery on record times the field's irrigation efficiency. What is left is ETAW met by groundwater.
  4. Divide ETAW met by groundwater by the field's irrigation efficiency. The result is the estimated groundwater extraction, which OpenH2O charges against the groundwater allocation.
  5. In a month with a meter reading on record, the reading stands. The same arithmetic runs for comparison but writes no groundwater row and no estimated extraction.
