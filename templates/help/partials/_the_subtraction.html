Groundwater consumed is what remains after the lines above it are subtracted; groundwater extracted is that figure divided by the share the crop consumes, and it is the figure the groundwater allocation is charged. Both are only as good as the delivery record above them.

{% else %}

Once a month has been calculated for a field with a well and no meter reading, this page works through that field's numbers. Until then, here is the arithmetic in words.

  1. Start with crop water use for the month, a satellite estimate in acre-feet.
  2. Subtract the rain the crop could use, estimated from weather data for the area.
  3. Subtract the canal water the crop could use: the delivery on record multiplied by the field's irrigation efficiency.
  4. What is left is groundwater consumed.
  5. Divide it by the share of pumped groundwater the crop consumes, 80% unless changed on Delivery Settings; the result is groundwater extracted, which the groundwater allocation is charged.
  6. In a month with a meter reading on record, the reading stands; the same arithmetic runs for comparison but writes no groundwater row and no extracted figure.
