From a zone’s ceiling to an account’s allocation

Topic How it works
Ceilings Each allocation ceiling covers one zone, one water type and one water year. An administrator or operator enters it on the {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Allocations{% else %}Allocations{% endif %} screen.
An account’s share OpenH2O divides a zone’s groundwater ceiling among its accounts by acreage: each account receives the part of the ceiling that its use areas’ acres are of all the acres in the zone.
A zone with no acreage on record When every use area in a zone lacks an area on record, OpenH2O divides the zone’s ceiling equally among its use areas.
More than one zone An account with use areas in several zones receives the sum of its shares from each zone.

Worked example

Figure Value
The zone’s groundwater ceiling 12,000 acre-feet
Acres of use areas in the zone 50,000
Acres of the account’s use areas in the zone 3,000
The account’s groundwater allocation 12,000 × 3,000 / 50,000 = 720 acre-feet

What counts against an allocation

{% if 'wells' in enabled_modules %}{% endif %} {% if 'wells' in enabled_modules %}{% endif %}
Topic How it works
Groundwater extraction Groundwater extraction on an account’s use areas counts against its allocation. OpenH2O uses the meter reading for a well with a meter and its own estimate for a well without one. An entry added with New entry or Upload CSV counts only when its water type is groundwater, or when it is a meter reading with no water type.
Surface water deliveries OpenH2O counts surface water deliveries only against the zone’s surface water ceiling, which it leaves undivided. OpenH2O shows the ceiling and the deliveries side by side on the zone’s page.
Remaining allocation An account’s remaining allocation is its allocation, plus any amount carried forward to it and any credit listed on its page, minus the groundwater extraction on its use areas. A negative figure means the account has extracted more than that.
Carry-over When the rollover_allocations command is run for a water year that closed, OpenH2O carries each zone’s and each account’s remaining groundwater allocation into the next year, a debt as well as a surplus. A zone set to let a surplus expire carries only a debt. Surface water is not carried forward.
A zone with no ceiling When none of the zones an account’s use areas lie in has a groundwater ceiling for the water year, OpenH2O shows a dash for that account’s allocation.
