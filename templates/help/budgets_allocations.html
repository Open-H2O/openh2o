The ceiling and the allocation

An allocation ceiling is the total volume assigned to a management zone for a reporting period. An allocation is one account’s share of it, by the land the account holds in the zone.

Figure What it is Who sets it One per
Allocation ceiling The total volume assigned to a zone for a period. You, on {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Allocations{% else %}Allocations{% endif %} Zone, water type and reporting period
Allocation One account’s share: the ceiling pro-rated by the use areas the account holds in the zone. Use areas are the fields on the Use Areas page. The platform Account, from the groundwater ceiling of every zone it holds use areas in

How an account’s allocation is worked out

Step Figure in the example Where the figure comes from
1Zone ceiling for the year 12,000 acre-feet Set on Allocations for the zone, its groundwater type and the period
2Use areas in the zone 600 Counted on the Use Areas page, one per field in the zone; the count, not the acreage
3The account’s use areas in the zone 30 Counted the same way, for the fields the account holds in the zone
4The account’s allocation 12,000 × 30 / 600 = 600 acre-feet Step 1 times step 3, divided by step 2; added up over every zone the account holds use areas in

A landowner’s figure is the account’s allocation, not the zone’s 12,000 acre-feet.

The count of use areas is used, not their acreage, because area data is often incomplete and counts are always available.

What spends it, and what is left

Allocation Spent by Remaining
Groundwater Groundwater extracted, metered and calculated alike (the meter reading, or the division{% if 'wells' in enabled_modules %} for a well with no meter{% endif %}) Allocation plus any carry-over, minus groundwater extracted
Surface, a district’s own Delivered, added up over the fields in the district; a surface ceiling is not divided among accounts Allocation minus delivered
Blank, no ceiling set Nothing; no ceiling is set for the zone and period, so every account’s allocation shows blank, not zero Blank; not a limit

When an account pumps past its allocation, remaining goes negative: an overdraft, the one figure the platform colors red.

“No ceiling defined” and “a ceiling of zero” are different: a blank is not a limit.

To start enforcing allocations, set a ceiling for the zone and period on {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Allocations{% else %}Allocations{% endif %} first.
