Methodology Settings

Setting What it does
Calculation steps OpenH2O runs the calculation steps in the order {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Methodology Settings{% else %}Methodology Settings{% endif %} lists them. By default, all five steps are on, in the order {% if show_methods %}Methods behind the numbers{% else %}Methods behind the numbers{% endif %} describes. An administrator can reorder them or switch any of them off.
Effective precipitation Effective precipitation can be all of the month’s rain, a fixed percentage of it, or an estimate by the USDA-SCS method. The default is the USDA-SCS method. Its default soil moisture storage is 3.0 inches, and the default fixed percentage is 70 percent.
Lowest remainder OpenH2O subtracts effective precipitation and the delivery times irrigation efficiency from crop ET. The result never falls below this value. The default is 0 acre-feet.

Delivery Settings

Setting What it does
Default irrigation efficiency A field with no irrigation method on file uses this efficiency, which is 75 percent by default. A field with an irrigation method on file uses the efficiency of its method. A field uses the same efficiency for its surface water and its groundwater.
Surface water in excess of ETAW By default, OpenH2O gives no credit for surface water in excess of ETAW. An administrator can instead show it as a separate line on the field’s page without crediting it, or credit it to the landowner after leaving a share in the basin. That share is 10 percent by default.{% if 'wells' in enabled_modules %} A field with a well receives the credit on its own ledger, and a field without a well has it added to its zone’s shared account.{% endif %}
Unused allocation By default, OpenH2O carries each zone’s unused allocation into the next water year as a credit. An administrator can instead let it expire at the end of the water year, and can set a different choice for one zone on that zone’s page. An overdrawn allocation is always carried into the next water year as a debt.
Reporting year Some diversion record files give a year and a month but no date. OpenH2O reads that year as a reporting year to place each record in a calendar month. The reporting year can be a water year starting in October, which is the default, a calendar year, or a season starting in a month an administrator chooses.
Record addresses OpenH2O publishes each record’s permanent address under the web address entered in this field. When the field is blank, OpenH2O uses this site’s own address. This setting changes no figure.

Server configuration

Setting What it does
Access control Access control is on unless the server’s configuration switches it off. While it is on, only an administrator can change Methodology Settings and Delivery Settings, and people cannot create their own accounts. While it is off, anyone who is signed in can change them.

When a change takes effect

Change When it takes effect
A setting that changes a figure Each calculated month keeps the settings it was calculated with. A changed setting affects a past month only when that month is recalculated.
The year-end choice OpenH2O writes the carry-over when the rollover_allocations command is run on the server for the water year that closed. A changed choice applies from the next run.
The reporting year A changed reporting year applies to files imported after the change. Records already imported keep their months.
