On Methodology Settings

Setting What it does
Calculation steps OpenH2O runs the steps on {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Methodology Settings{% else %}Methodology Settings{% endif %} in the order listed. An administrator can reorder them or switch any of them off.
Effective precipitation The method can count all of the month’s rain, a fixed percentage of it (70 percent by default), or use the USDA-SCS method, which is the default. The USDA-SCS method assumes 3.0 inches of soil moisture storage unless an administrator changes it.
Lowest remainder The remainder after effective precipitation and surface water cannot fall below this value, which is 0 acre-feet by default.

On Delivery Settings

{% if 'wells' in enabled_modules %}{% endif %}
Setting What it does
Default irrigation efficiency A field with no irrigation method on file uses this efficiency, 75 percent unless an administrator changes it on {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings{% else %}Delivery Settings{% endif %}. A field with an irrigation method on file uses the efficiency of its method. Each field has one efficiency for its surface water and its groundwater.
Surface water in excess of ETAW By default, OpenH2O credits that water to no one. An administrator can instead credit it to the landowner, less a share left in the basin (10 percent by default), or show it as a separate line on the field’s page without crediting it.
Where a credit goes When surface water in excess of ETAW is credited, a field with a well receives the credit on its own ledger. A field without a well has the credit added to its zone’s shared account.
Unused allocation At the end of a water year, OpenH2O carries each zone’s unused allocation into the next water year as a credit, unless the setting lets unused allocation expire. An overdrawn allocation is always carried into the next water year as a debt.
One zone An administrator can set a different year-end choice for one zone on that zone’s page.
Reporting year When a diversion record file gives a year and a month but no date, this setting decides which calendar month each record belongs to: a water year starting in October (the default), a calendar year, or a season starting in a month an administrator chooses.
Record addresses This setting sets the web address under which each record’s permanent address is published. It changes no figure.

In the server’s configuration

Setting What it does
Sign-in Sign-in is required unless the server’s configuration turns it off. While it is required, only an administrator can change the settings on Methodology Settings and Delivery Settings. It changes no figure.

When a change takes effect

Change When it takes effect
A setting that changes a figure Each calculated month keeps the settings it was calculated with. A changed setting affects a past month only when that month is recalculated.
The year-end choice OpenH2O writes the carry-over when the rollover_allocations command is run for the water year that closed. A changed choice applies the next time that happens.
The reporting year A changed reporting year applies to files imported after the change. Records already imported keep their months.
