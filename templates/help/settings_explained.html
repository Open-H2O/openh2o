Methodology Settings

Setting What it sets Default
Calculation steps The order of the calculation steps, and which of them are switched on. {% if show_methods %}All five steps, switched on, in the order Methods behind the numbers describes.{% else %}All five steps, switched on.{% endif %}
Effective precipitation method All of the month’s rain, a fixed percentage of it, or the USDA-SCS method. The USDA-SCS method
Fixed percentage The share of the month’s rain counted under the fixed percentage method. 70 percent
Soil moisture storage The soil moisture storage the USDA-SCS method assumes. 3.0 inches
Lowest remainder The lowest value that crop ET less effective precipitation and the delivery times irrigation efficiency can reach. 0 acre-feet

Delivery Settings

Setting What it sets Default
Default irrigation efficiency The efficiency of a field with no irrigation method on file. A field with an irrigation method on file uses the efficiency of its method, for both its surface water and its groundwater. 75 percent
Surface water in excess of ETAW Not credited to anyone; shown on the field’s page as its own line; or credited to the landowner, less a share left in the basin.{% if 'wells' in enabled_modules %} A credit goes to the field’s own ledger when the field has a well, and to its zone’s shared account when it does not.{% endif %} Not credited to anyone
Share left in the basin The share of surface water in excess of ETAW left in the basin when the rest is credited to the landowner. 10 percent
Unused allocation at year-end Carried into the next water year as a credit, or left to expire. Each zone can have its own choice, set on the zone’s page. An overdrawn allocation is always carried into the next water year as a debt. Carried into the next water year
Reporting year For a diversion record file that gives a year and a month but no date, the year the file uses: a water year starting in October, a calendar year, or a season starting in a chosen month. Water year
Record addresses The web address under which each record’s permanent address is published. This site’s own address

Server configuration

Setting What it sets Default
Access control Whether only an administrator can change Methodology Settings and Delivery Settings. While access control is on, people also cannot create their own accounts. While it is off, anyone who is signed in can change those settings, except a viewer. On

When a change takes effect

Change When it takes effect
A setting that changes a figure Each calculated month keeps the settings it was calculated with. A changed setting affects a past month only when that month is recalculated.
The year-end choice OpenH2O writes the carry-over when the rollover_allocations command is run on the server for the water year that closed. A changed choice applies from the next run.
The reporting year A changed reporting year applies to files imported after the change. Records already imported keep their months.
