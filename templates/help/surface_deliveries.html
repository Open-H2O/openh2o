Surface water

{% if 'wells' in enabled_modules %}{% endif %}
Topic How it works
Irrigation efficiency Each field’s irrigation efficiency comes from its irrigation method on file. A field with no irrigation method on file uses the default efficiency on {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings{% else %}Delivery Settings{% endif %}, 75 percent unless the district changes it.
Delivery lost to efficiency OpenH2O records the share of a surface water delivery lost to irrigation efficiency as deep percolation and does not charge or credit any account for it.
The surplus When effective precipitation and the delivery times irrigation efficiency together exceed crop ET, OpenH2O sets the remainder to zero and records the surplus in two parts: effective precipitation in excess of crop ET, and surface water in excess of ETAW.
Surface water in excess of ETAW A setting on {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings{% else %}Delivery Settings{% endif %} determines how OpenH2O treats surface water in excess of ETAW. By default, OpenH2O credits that water to no one. A district can instead credit it to the landowner, less a share left in the basin (10 percent by default), or show it as a separate line on the field’s page without crediting it.
Where a credit goes When surface water in excess of ETAW is credited, a field with a well receives the credit on its own ledger. A field without a well has the credit added to its zone’s shared account.
Past months Each calculated month stores the settings it was calculated with. A changed setting affects a past month only when that month is recalculated.

Unused allocation at the end of the water year

Topic How it works
Unused allocation At the end of a water year, OpenH2O carries each zone’s unused allocation into the next water year as a credit, unless {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings{% else %}Delivery Settings{% endif %} is set to let unused allocation expire.
Overdrawn allocation OpenH2O always carries an overdrawn allocation into the next water year as a debt, whichever choice is set.
One zone A district can set a different choice for one zone on that zone’s page.
When it is written OpenH2O writes the carry-over when the rollover_allocations command is run for the water year that closed.
