The short version

When a district delivers canal water to its farms, two questions decide how the platform books that water. First, what share of a delivery the crop consumes. Second, whether a district's unused surface water carries into the next year or expires. You answer both once, on the {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings page{% else %}Delivery Settings page{% endif %}, and they apply to every district.

Setting 1 — how much of a delivery the crop uses

This setting is the share of a delivery the crop consumes; the rest is counted as recharge. A typical figure is 75%, meaning three-quarters is taken up by the plants and the remaining quarter returns to the groundwater as recharge.

Why it matters: the platform splits a district's monthly delivery across its fields by each field's ET that month, so a summer crop takes a larger share than a fallow field. This percentage caps how much any one field can be credited with using, so a delivery is shared sensibly instead of split evenly regardless of what’s growing.

Setting 2 — what happens to unused water at year-end

A district has a surface-water allotment for the water year. If it doesn’t use the whole thing by the time the year closes, you choose what the platform does with the leftover:

Carry it forward default

The unused amount carries into next year as a credit.

Let it expire use-it-or-lose-it

The leftover expires at year-end; next year starts at zero.

A debt always carries

If a district overdraws — uses more than its allotment — that shortfall always carries into next year as an obligation, no matter which choice above is set. An overdraw is a real debt; only a surplus is governed by the carry-forward-or-expire choice.

Who sets these — and when

These are agency policy, not farmer entry. An analyst or administrator sets them once during setup and revisits them rarely — for example, if the agency adopts a different efficiency assumption, or its board changes the year-end rule. A farmer never touches them. They enter every future delivery calculation; change them the way you would the methodology.

When one district needs to differ

The year-end rule is set agency-wide, but a single district can be flipped to a different choice without changing everyone else. Open that district’s page and use the Year-end unused water control: leave it on “Use agency default” for the common case, or set it to carry-forward or expire just for that district. Switching it back to the agency default means that district will follow along automatically if you later change the agency-wide rule.

Set it per district under Zones, or change the agency-wide defaults on the {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings{% else %}Delivery Settings{% endif %} page.
