How a delivery is counted

Amount How it is found Where it goes
Canal water the crop could use Delivered, multiplied by the field's irrigation efficiency, from its irrigation method on file, else the agency default on Delivery Settings, 75% unless changed Subtracted from crop water use after rain
The rest of the delivery, lost to irrigation efficiency Delivered minus canal water the crop could use Recorded as deep percolation on the field's water balance; not crop water use, not a credit, not charged
Canal water beyond what the crop could use Canal water the crop could use that went past what was left of crop water use after rain By the choice in the next table

An administrator sets the agency default on {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings{% else %}Delivery Settings{% endif %}, as “Share of delivered water the crop consumes”; a field's irrigation method, set on the field's own page, takes its place for that field.

Canal water beyond what the crop could use

Choice on Delivery Settings What is recorded Who is credited
Not credited, the default The amount is recorded with the month's figures and nothing else happens. No one
Credited to the landowner, less a share left in the basin The amount, and the landowner's share of it after “Share left in the basin” is subtracted, 10% unless changed {% if 'wells' in enabled_modules %}Fields with a well, the landowner, on the field's own ledger; fields with no well, the zone's shared account, or no one where the zone has none{% else %}The zone's shared account, or no one where the zone has none{% endif %}
Its own line, not a credit The amount, as a line of its own on the field's page No one, and nothing is charged; the field's water balance reads the same as under the other two choices

Each month keeps the choice and the share it was calculated under; a change applies to months calculated after it.

Unused allotment at the end of the water year

Choice What happens to the unused amount What happens to an overdraw
Carry it forward as a credit toward next year, the default The unused amount carries into next year as a credit. Carried into next year as a debt
Let it expire (use-it-or-lose-it) The leftover expires at year-end; next year starts at zero. Carried into next year as a debt, whichever choice is set

An administrator sets the agency's choice on {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings{% else %}Delivery Settings{% endif %}, as “When a zone doesn't use its full allocation by the end of the water year”.

One zone can differ

“Year-end unused water” on the zone’s page under Zones What that zone follows
Use agency default The agency’s choice on Delivery Settings, and any later change to it
Carry it forward as a credit Carried forward for this zone only, whatever the agency’s choice
Let it expire (use-it-or-lose-it) Expires for this zone only, whatever the agency’s choice
