How the two groundwater figures are worked out

The steps, in the order they run

Every field goes through the same five steps each month, and every figure is in acre-feet.

Action Where the number comes from Setting that changes it
1Start from crop water use The satellite estimate of what the crop used this month. None
2Subtract the rain the crop could use Rain estimated from weather data for the area, then the share the crop could use by the method chosen: all of it, a fixed fraction of it, or the USDA-SCS soil-storage method, the default. {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Methodology Settings{% else %}Methodology Settings{% endif %}, the rain method
3Subtract the canal water the crop could use The delivery on record times the field's irrigation efficiency, because not all the canal water reaches the crop: some soaks past the roots and some runs off the end of the field. {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings{% else %}Delivery Settings{% endif %}, the default irrigation efficiency (75% unless changed) for a field with no irrigation method on file
4Set what is left to zero where it is below zero, and to zero on a field with no crop Two plan steps. Where rain and canal water together exceed crop water use, the excess is recorded in two parts: rain the crop did not need, and canal water beyond what the crop could use. {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Methodology Settings{% else %}Methodology Settings{% endif %}, the floor
5Divide groundwater consumed by the share of pumped groundwater the crop consumes What is left after step 4 is groundwater consumed; divided by the share, 80% unless changed, it is groundwater extracted, the figure the groundwater allocation is charged. {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings{% else %}Delivery Settings{% endif %}, the share of pumped groundwater the crop consumes

Steps 1 to 4 are the calculation plan on Methodology Settings, where they can be reordered and switched on or off; step 5 runs after the plan.

Step 3 has a switch in the plan, on unless changed. When it is off, the whole delivery is subtracted. The switch is not yet on the Methodology Settings screen.

A pump has to lift more water than the crop ends up using, because some water soaks past the roots. Step 5 allows for that, and the difference is recorded on the month's calculation as deep percolation.

Every month calculated carries the plan and the settings it ran under, so a changed setting moves no month already run.

Methodology Settings opens from the links in this table and from the Dashboard; Delivery Settings opens from the links in this table.

How the month ends, by what the field has

Outcome A meter reading A well, no meter No well
Groundwater consumed The reading, as recorded What is left after step 4; 0.00 acre-feet where rain and canal water covered the crop None
Groundwater extracted, the figure the groundwater allocation is charged The reading Groundwater consumed divided by the share the crop consumes, from step 5 Nothing
What the field's page shows The reading, with the calculation kept beside it for comparison The month's calculation, line by line Water use recorded, no supply reported

Water use recorded, no supply reported says only that the supplies on record did not cover crop water use; it is never written as pumping, and it is never a finding about the grower.

Canal water the crops did not use

Two different amounts, each recorded on the month's calculation.

Amount How it is found What happens to it
Lost to irrigation efficiency Delivered minus canal water the crop could use; 25% of the delivery at a 75% efficiency Recorded as deep percolation on the field's water balance; not crop use, not a credit, and not charged to anyone
Canal water beyond what the crop could use Canal water the crop could use that went past what was left of crop water use after rain One setting on {% if 'accounting' in enabled_modules %}Delivery Settings{% else %}Delivery Settings{% endif %} decides, with the three values in the next table
Value on Delivery Settings What is recorded Who is credited
Not credited, the default The amount, on the month's calculation No one
Credited to the landowner, less a share left in the basin The amount, and the share credited after 10% (unless changed) is left in the basin A field with a well, on its own ledger; a field with no well, its zone's shared account; a field with no such zone, no one
Its own line The amount, as a line of its own on the field's page No one, and nothing is charged

The field's water balance reads the same under all three values, and each month keeps the value it was calculated under.

How one headgate total is shared

A district records what left each headgate, not what reached each field, so each month the software divides every headgate total among the fields it serves.

{% include "help/partials/_canal_split_diagram.html" %}

When no field on the headgate has crop water use after rain on record for the month, each field gets the fixed share on file for it instead. Every field's ledger row names the rule that set its share.

A diversion with no meter

Where a point of diversion has no meter in service and no volume entered for the month, the software estimates that month's delivery.

Step Rule
1Estimate the delivery from the fields Each field's crop water use after rain divided by its irrigation efficiency, added up, then increased to allow for the canal's losses.
2Cap it At what is left of the water right's face value for the water year; zero in a month outside the right's season.
3Record it as an estimate A provisional record, labeled an estimate on the canal's page; a volume entered for the month replaces it.

No estimate is made under a curtailed water right or where no water right is linked.

Crop water use above the cap stays in the field's remainder and ends the way the table above says: on a field with a well it is groundwater consumed, and on a field without one it is water use recorded, no supply reported.

What this platform does not do yet

  • A flag when an estimate reaches the face value. The estimate is capped today; a flag on the water right's page, for the district to review, is not yet built.
  • A flat yearly rain allowance. Rain is subtracted month by month, as in step 2; a single yearly allowance is not yet built.

OpenH2O does not submit reports to the state.
