ISS-099: "All clear" is a CLAIM, and it is only true if the exception counters above it could have seen a problem. When the calculation engine has never run, every consumptive-use figure on the page is 0.00 for want of a measurement, so `accounts_over_budget` is structurally zero — no account can exceed its allocation by consuming nothing. The strip therefore has nothing to report and says so, which read as reassurance on staging for two days.
