Cell 1 is what the field's month came to, by the run's own stamped disposition: the consumed figure on a field with a well (the calculated ledger entry; kept for comparison on a metered field, where the meter is the groundwater figure), or, on a field with no well, the remainder recorded as water use with no supply on record.
