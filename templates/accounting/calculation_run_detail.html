{% extends "base.html" %} {% load humanize %} {% load placeholders %} {% comment %} The calculation page for one field and one month. Staff open it when a landowner questions the groundwater charged on an unmetered well for one month, to confirm each input and setting or find the wrong one. Only a month on a field with a well and no meter reading has that question (metered means a meter_reading ledger row in the month, run_calculations.py), so the equations render only when the run's residual_disposition is "groundwater". A metered or no-well month reached by a direct URL gets the short page at the foot of this file: one plain sentence and a link back to the field. The page is the dashboard's equation panel (.budget-panel--centered), with three equations in the engineer's terms: Crop ET - Effective precipitation = ETAW; ETAW - ETAW met by surface water = ETAW met by groundwater; ETAW met by groundwater / Groundwater irrigation efficiency = Groundwater extraction (estimated). Each figure carries its source and setting beneath it as a label and a figure, never a sentence, and no sentence writes the arithmetic out. Rain is gridMET's (steps.py subtract_effective_precip); crop water use is OpenET's (steps.py et_gross). Every figure is the run's own stamped column, and both percentages are read back off the run (the view's docstring says how), never off the live Delivery Settings value.
