Columns each layout carries

The layout is recognized by its headers; other columns are ignored.

The state’s eAR production export

Column Required What it holds
PWSID Yes Public Water System Identification number; must match the water system on file
Year Yes Report year
Month Yes Month name, January to December
DateStartOfMonth No First day of the month, M/D/YYYY; read when Month is not a month name
TypeCode Yes Source, one of the state’s seven: GW, SW, Purchased, Sold, Recycled, NonPotable, NonPotableSold; any other code is a row error
Units of Measure As Reported No G, MG, AF or CCF (gallons, million gallons, acre-feet, hundred cubic feet); a blank cell is a row error until a unit for blank rows is chosen on the preview
Quantity as in Units Reported Yes The month’s volume, in that unit

The operator’s monthly log

One column per source; the report year and one unit for the whole file, gallons unless changed, are chosen on the preview.

Column Required What it holds
Date/Month Yes Month name; a row that is not a month, such as Maximum Day, Annual Total or Percent Treated, is skipped and named
Water Produced from Groundwater (Wells) One source at least Recorded as Groundwater
Water Produced from Surface Water One source at least Recorded as Surface water
Finished Water Purchased or Received from another PWS One source at least Recorded as Purchased
Water Sold to Another PWS One source at least Recorded as Sold
Non-potable (exclude recycled) One source at least Recorded as Non-potable
Recycled One source at least Recorded as Recycled
Total Amount of Potable Water No Never read; it totals the source columns above
