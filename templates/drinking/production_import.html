Columns OpenH2O reads

The eAR export

Column Required What it holds
PWSID Yes The Public Water System ID. OpenH2O imports only rows whose PWSID matches the water system on file.
Year Yes Report year
Month Yes Month name, January to December
DateStartOfMonth No The first day of the month, written M/D/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD. OpenH2O reads this column when Month is not a month name.
TypeCode Yes The type of water: GW, SW, Purchased, Sold, Recycled, NonPotable or NonPotableSold. OpenH2O refuses a row with any other code.
Units of Measure As Reported No G, MG, AF or CCF: gallons, million gallons, acre-feet or hundred cubic feet. When this cell is blank, choose the unit on the preview.
Quantity as in Units Reported Yes The month’s volume in the row’s unit

A monthly log on the eAR template

The monthly log has one column per type of water. On the preview, enter the report year and choose one unit for the whole file. Gallons is the default.

Column Required What it holds
Date/Month Yes The month name. OpenH2O skips summary rows such as Maximum Day, Annual Total and Percent Treated, and lists those rows on the preview.
Water Produced from Groundwater (Wells) At least one of the six Recorded as Groundwater
Water Produced from Surface Water At least one of the six Recorded as Surface water
Finished Water Purchased or Received from another PWS At least one of the six Recorded as Purchased
Water Sold to Another PWS At least one of the six Recorded as Sold
Non-potable (exclude recycled) At least one of the six Recorded as Non-potable
Recycled At least one of the six Recorded as Recycled
Total Amount of Potable Water No The sum of groundwater, surface water and purchased water. OpenH2O does not read this column.
