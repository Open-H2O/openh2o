What From EPA Left for you
Water system Name, status, system type, owner type, primary source and mailing address. Look the system up again and press Refresh to update these fields from EPA. State classification and regulating agency. OpenH2O fills a blank regulating agency from the Regulating Agency column of an imported lab file.
Facilities Each facility EPA carries with a state-assigned facility ID Nothing. OpenH2O leaves out a facility with no state-assigned facility ID and names the facility in the review.
Sampling points None; EPA's record carries none Each point your lab file names, added after you confirm
Population and service connections EPA's total for each The counts by category. EPA sends one total for population and one for connections. An administrator enters the counts by category at /admin/ under Drinking Water > Water Systems.
Compliance Nothing OpenH2O does not determine compliance.
Links to wells None You link a facility to its well on the facility's page under Facilities, with Edit local name. Refresh never changes a link you have made.
