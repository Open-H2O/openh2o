Reporting / Reports / Shared-supply check

When one well or canal feeds several fields, someone sets each field's share of that water by hand. This page compares each hand-entered share against the share that estimated satellite crop-water use (ET) would imply, and flags any field where the two are far apart — a likely typo worth a look on the source's own page. It only points out where to check; it never changes a split.

Shared-Supply Check

Demo

This demo mixes invented sample data with real published records to show what the platform can do. See the difference. For testing purposes only.

8 hand-set shared sources · 3 flagged · gap threshold 15 points

A field is flagged when its entered share differs from its ET-implied share by 15 or more percentage points. This only checks how a shared source's water is split between its fields — it is not about whether each field's overall water balance closes, so a flagged field can still have a perfectly balanced ledger (it just draws a different amount from this source than its crop-water use alone would suggest).
