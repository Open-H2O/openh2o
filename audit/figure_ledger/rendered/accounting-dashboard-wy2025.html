How this summary works: Consumptive use, estimated here from satellite evapotranspiration (ET), is the demand. It is met by three supplies: surface-water deliveries, groundwater pumping, and effective rainfall. Each area's consumptive use is shown below beside the supplies that met it; the balance is the supplies minus consumptive use.
